Privacy Policy

Ankit FAS

Effective Date: October 5, 2026 Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Ankit FAS ("the App") is an attendance management application designed to manage student information, fingerprint-based attendance, and related administrative activities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is stored and used, and how it is protected.

Important: Fingerprint templates used by Ankit FAS are stored locally on the Android device. They are not uploaded to an Ankit FAS server or remote database.

1. Information We Handle

Student Information

The App may store information entered by an authorized administrator, including:

  • Student name
  • Mobile number
  • Guardian name
  • Guardian mobile number
  • Batch information
  • Batch year
  • Other information required for attendance management

Fingerprint Information

Ankit FAS supports fingerprint-based student enrollment and attendance using compatible fingerprint hardware.

The App may create and store a fingerprint template associated with a student. Fingerprint templates:

  • Are stored locally on the Android device.
  • Are used for fingerprint enrollment and attendance identification.
  • Are not uploaded to an Ankit FAS server.
  • Are not transmitted to a remote database by the App.
  • Are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • Are not used for advertising or profiling.

The App does not store a photographic image of a fingerprint as part of the normal fingerprint enrollment process. It stores the fingerprint template required by the supported fingerprint hardware and software.

2. How We Use Information

Information stored by Ankit FAS may be used to:

  • Register and manage students.
  • Manage batches and academic information.
  • Enroll fingerprints for attendance identification.
  • Record student attendance.
  • Search and manage attendance records.
  • Contact students or guardians by SMS when the SMS feature is used.
  • Generate attendance-related reports.
  • Perform administrative functions within the App.

The information is not used for advertising or targeted marketing.

3. Fingerprint Data Storage

Fingerprint templates are stored only on the Android device on which they are enrolled.

Ankit FAS does not upload fingerprint templates to a cloud service or remote server.

If the device is lost, damaged, reset, or its application data is deleted, locally stored fingerprint templates may become unavailable unless the administrator has made an appropriate backup using functionality provided by the App.

Deleting a student's fingerprint record from the App removes the corresponding locally stored fingerprint information from the App's local storage and/or supported fingerprint device, as applicable.

4. SMS

Ankit FAS may provide functionality to send SMS messages to students or guardians.

SMS messages are sent directly from the Android device using the device's mobile network/SMS capability.

Ankit FAS does not use a separate SMS server or SMS gateway to send these messages.

The App may use the phone number stored for a student or guardian to address an SMS when the administrator chooses to send one.

Normal mobile-network or carrier charges may apply to SMS messages according to the user's mobile service provider.

The App does not use SMS functionality to send advertising or promotional messages.

5. Local Storage

Ankit FAS may use local storage, including a local SQLite database, to store application information such as:

  • Student records
  • Batch information
  • Attendance records
  • Fingerprint templates or references required for fingerprint attendance
  • Application settings

This information is stored on the device unless a particular feature explicitly provides for another form of data transfer.

6. Data Sharing

Ankit FAS does not sell or rent personal information.

Fingerprint templates are not shared with third parties.

The App does not intentionally transmit fingerprint templates to external servers.

Information may be disclosed if required by applicable law, legal process, or a lawful government request.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored by the App.

Because some information is stored locally on the Android device, users should also protect the device using appropriate security measures such as:

  • Device screen lock
  • PIN or password
  • Biometric device authentication
  • Keeping Android and the App updated
  • Restricting access to authorized personnel

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain stored on the device for as long as it is required for attendance and administrative purposes.

Authorized users can delete student information, attendance information, and fingerprint records using the functionality provided by the App.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its application data may delete locally stored application information, subject to data that may have been exported or backed up separately.

Fingerprint templates stored on supported external fingerprint hardware may require separate deletion through the App or device management functionality.

9. Children's Information

Ankit FAS may be used to manage attendance information for students who may be under the age of 18.

The App is intended to be used by authorized educational or administrative personnel rather than directly by children.

Student information should only be entered and processed by authorized personnel with the appropriate authority to manage such information.

10. Permissions

Depending on the features being used, Ankit FAS may request permissions necessary to:

  • Send SMS messages from the device.
  • Communicate with supported fingerprint hardware.
  • Access device functionality required for attendance management.
  • Store application data locally.

Permissions are used only for the functionality for which they are required.

11. Third-Party Services

Ankit FAS does not use third-party advertising networks to display targeted advertisements.

The App does not sell personal information to third parties.

The Android operating system, mobile network provider, Google Play, or hardware manufacturers may independently process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality, data handling practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

Any updated version will include a revised effective date.
